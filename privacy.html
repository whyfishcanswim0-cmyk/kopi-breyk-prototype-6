Privacy notice

Last updated: November 22, 2026

Who we are

Kopi Breyk (“we”) runs this website and the Kopi Breyk Rewards app. This notice explains how we handle your personal information, in line with the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173).

What we collect

Your username and PIN (the PIN is stored scrambled, so we cannot read it), full name, mobile number, birthday, optional email, a profile photo, your purchase records (receipt number, amount, points), visit dates, free spins and prizes, feedback you send, reservations and event RSVPs, and referral information.

Why we collect it

To run the rewards program (points, spins, streaks), to confirm at the counter that an account belongs to you (your photo), to greet you on your birthday, to handle reservations and events, to prevent fraud and duplicate accounts, and to improve our service. If you agreed to it, we may also send you offers.

Who can see it

Only authorized Kopi Breyk staff. Your data is stored with our service providers: Supabase (database) and Vercel (website hosting). We do not sell your information.

Community

If you join the community, other community members can see your nickname, community picture, posts, photos, comments and likes. Your security photo, real name, mobile number and birthday are never shown. Posts may be reviewed by staff before they appear, and we may remove content that breaks our guidelines. The community is for members aged 13 and up, and photo posts are for members aged 18 and up. You can leave the community at any time in the app, which deletes your community profile, posts, comments and likes.

How long we keep it

While your account is active. When you delete your account, your personal details, photo, feedback, reservations and RSVPs are removed, and your points are forfeited. We keep sales records without your name for accounting and tax purposes.

Your rights

You may ask to see, correct, or delete your information, object to how it is used, or withdraw your consent at any time. You can delete your account yourself in the app under Profile. You may also file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission (privacy.gov.ph).

Contact us

Please ask any of our staff.
