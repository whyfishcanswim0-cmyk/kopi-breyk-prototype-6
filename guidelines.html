Community guidelines

Last updated: November 22, 2026

Be kind

Treat everyone the way you would at the cafe. No insults, bullying, hate, threats, or harassment.

Respect privacy

Don’t post anyone’s photo, name, number, or personal details without their permission. Don’t share your own private information either.

Keep it appropriate

No adult, violent, or illegal content, no spam or advertising, and no pretending to be staff or another person.

Photos

Photo posts are for members aged 18 and up. Post only photos you took or have permission to share.

How we moderate

Posts may be reviewed before they appear. Anyone can report a post or comment, and our team can remove content and turn off posting for members who break these rules.

Questions or problems

Please ask any of our staff.
